## El Salvador payout policy
Boltech must evaluate payout mechanics before committing to any affiliate program.

Preferred order:
1. Direct bank / low-cost supported payout
2. Bitcoin/crypto payout through a compliant provider where officially supported
3. Payoneer/Wise when the partner explicitly supports them
4. PayPal only when necessary
5. Do not create a paid software subscription merely to qualify for an affiliate relationship

For PartnerStack-based programs, PayPal is currently the practical payout route for Boltech in El Salvador unless PartnerStack expands local direct-deposit support.
