# Stock below minimum with a suggested reorder

Status: APPROVED (2026-11-05) · Suggested offer: custom-agent · Price: quoted after scope review

## Problem

Inventory is checked by eye; stockouts appear, followed by more expensive emergency purchases.

## What we deliver

- Daily list of items below minimum
- Suggested quantity based on consumption
- Supplier linked to each item
- Human approval of the reorder

## Acceptance criteria

Thirty simulated days with no stockouts on items that have a defined minimum, with the suggestion reviewable on one screen.

## What we do not promise

We do not promise savings; the result is measured against the agreed criteria. The suggestion depends on the minimum being defined.

## Fifteen-minute demo

A sample inventory is loaded, one item drops below minimum, and the alert appears with the suggested quantity.
