# Orders from email and WhatsApp into one table

Status: APPROVED (2026-11-05) · Suggested offer: implementation · Price: quoted after scope review

## Problem

Orders arrive by email and WhatsApp, someone retypes them by hand, and they get lost, duplicated or logged late.

## What we deliver

- Captured from the original message
- One unique number per order
- Human confirmation for ambiguous cases
- Status visible to the whole team

## Acceptance criteria

Twenty test orders logged with no duplicates and no losses, at under two minutes per order.

## What we do not promise

We do not promise that every data-entry mistake disappears; we do promise every order is logged and traceable.

## Fifteen-minute demo

A real sample email is captured live, and the unique number and status are shown.
