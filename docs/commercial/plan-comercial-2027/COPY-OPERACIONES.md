### 1. Pedidos que llegan por correo y WhatsApp

**Título:** Deja de copiar pedidos a mano
Cada pedido entra con número único, cliente, ítems y estado. Nadie vuelve a preguntar "¿este ya se registró?".
- Se registra desde el correo o el mensaje original.
- Los casos dudosos los confirma una persona.
- Se acabaron los pedidos duplicados y los que se pierden.

**Qué no prometemos:** que desaparezcan todos los errores de captura; sí que cada pedido queda registrado y rastreable.

### 2. Facturas contra orden de compra

**Título:** Detecta la diferencia antes de pagar
Comparamos cada factura con su orden de compra y te marcamos qué no coincide, con el monto.
- Cada diferencia aparece con su motivo.
- Finanzas aprueba o rechaza, no el sistema.
- El informe muestra cuánto dinero está en discusión.

**Qué no prometemos:** detectar facturas sin texto legible; eso se valida antes de cotizar.

### 3. Stock bajo mínimo

**Título:** Entérate del quiebre antes de que ocurra
Cada día ves qué ítems están por debajo del mínimo y cuánto conviene reponer.
- La cantidad sugerida se calcula con tu consumo.
- Operaciones aprueba la reposición.
- El sistema no compra ni paga nada.

**Qué no prometemos:** ahorro garantizado; el resultado se mide con el criterio de aceptación acordado.

# English

## Entry page

**Headline:** Three operations tasks you can automate this week
**Lead:** Pick the task that costs you the most time. You get a written assessment, no mandatory demo, and no price in the first conversation.
**Button:** Request a written assessment

### 1. Orders arriving by email and WhatsApp

**Headline:** Stop retyping orders by hand
Every order gets a unique number, customer, items and status. Nobody has to ask "was this one logged?" again.
- Captured from the original email or message.
- Ambiguous cases are confirmed by a person.
- No more duplicates and no more lost orders.

**What we do not promise:** that every data-entry mistake disappears; we do promise every order is logged and traceable.

### 2. Invoices against purchase orders

**Headline:** Catch the difference before you pay
Each invoice is matched to its purchase order and every mismatch is flagged with the amount.
- Every difference comes with a reason.
- Finance approves or rejects; the system does not decide.
- The report shows how much money is in dispute.

**What we do not promise:** reading invoices with no legible text; that is validated before quoting.

### 3. Stock below minimum

**Headline:** Find out about a stockout before it happens
Every day you see which items are below minimum and how much to reorder.
- Suggested quantity is based on your consumption.
- Operations approves the reorder.
- The system never buys or pays anything.

**What we do not promise:** guaranteed savings; results are measured against the agreed acceptance criteria.

## Closing

**Headline:** Start with one task, not a project
Pick one task, agree the scope in writing, and define how it will be measured. Price is quoted after the scope review.

**Button:** Request a written assessment
