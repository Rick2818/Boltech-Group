# Boltech Group — Partner Application Pack v1.0

## Company
**Boltech Group**

## What Boltech does
Boltech designs and implements custom autonomous AI agents for commercial, customer-support and operational workflows.

Core use cases:
- sales and lead-response agents
- customer-support agents
- WhatsApp agents
- voice agents
- quotation and follow-up workflows
- CRM integrations
- workflow automation
- custom business agents

## Market focus
Initial focus:
- El Salvador and Central America
- Spanish-speaking businesses
- bilingual opportunities in the United States

## Ideal client profile
SMBs and mid-market companies with:
- repetitive inbound questions
- slow lead response
- manual quotations
- high WhatsApp or support volume
- manual CRM follow-up
- operational bottlenecks that can be automated

## Partnership model sought
Boltech is interested in:
- referral partnerships
- affiliate relationships
- co-selling where appropriate
- technology integrations that improve customer outcomes

Initial commercial policy:
- no upfront partnership fee
- no mandatory paid software plan as a condition to participate where avoidable
- no minimum-sales commitment during validation
- commissions/revenue share tied to successful business
- customer data shared only after customer consent
- pricing, contracts and movement of money remain human-approved

## What Boltech can contribute
- qualified Central American and Spanish-speaking opportunities
- custom-agent implementation capability
- integration and workflow design
- local commercial context
- co-selling support
- transparent deal-registration and attribution tracking

## Operational controls
Boltech maintains:
- partner directory
- deal/referral registration
- consent gate before PII sharing
- partner activity log
- referral status tracking
- commission tracking
- human approval for commercial commitments

## Current partnership evaluation criteria
A partner is prioritized when:
1. joining is free or success-based
2. no minimum sales are required
3. terms are public and understandable
4. payout mechanics are defined
5. the relationship can support SMB/mid-market clients
6. Central America or global participation is possible
7. Boltech does not have to incur fixed cost before revenue

## Application-ready positioning
Short description:
> Boltech Group builds custom autonomous AI agents that solve sales, customer-support and workflow bottlenecks for businesses in Central America and Spanish-speaking markets. We are building a partner ecosystem for referral, co-selling and technology integrations, with formal deal registration, consent controls and transparent attribution.

## Guardrail
Affiliate programs are not treated as inbound lead sources for Boltech unless the partner explicitly offers reciprocal referral or co-selling. A vendor affiliate relationship means Boltech can refer customers to that vendor and earn a commission; it does not imply the vendor will send AI-agent clients to Boltech.
