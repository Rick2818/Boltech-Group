# Agent 1 — lead-verification rule, 2026-10-18
No synthetic record, guessed identity, unconfirmed mailbox, or incomplete buyer need may be handed to Agent 2 or used for outbound delivery.
Agent 1 must produce **exactly five unique complete buyer records**, replacing invalid candidates as necessary. A failed candidate such as unidentifiable **Marsyrand** counts as rejected, **not** one of the five. The gate explicitly returns RESEARCH_REPLACEMENTS until five qualify. Rejection does not authorize arbitrary bulk enrichment, sending or credit usage.
The gate evaluates evidence flags supplied by authoritative upstream verification. It cannot itself research the web, confirm mailbox ownership, infer consent, send email or guarantee five available buyers. Such claims require separate production evidence. Verification needs (a) automated tests, (b) a production source that sets evidence-backed flags, (c) an actual handoff receipt to Agent 2, (d) monitoring/retry with idempotency. Do not mark production autonomy certified without all four.
